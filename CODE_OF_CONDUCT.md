# Code of Conduct

CamAlert adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other project space.

In short: be welcoming, kind and patient, respect different opinions and levels of experience, give and accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks and publishing other people's private information (such as addresses, camera pictures or network
details) are not accepted.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, open a private report through the
[Security tab](https://github.com/kevinrossnl/camalert/security) › **Report a vulnerability** and start the title
with *Code of Conduct*. Only the maintainers can see it. Reports are handled confidentially; the maintainers
decide on the consequences following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
